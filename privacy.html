JARVIS Content Creator

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how JARVIS Content Creator, operated by Kevin.Kools.Digital, handles information when connecting to supported third-party platforms and APIs.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the services authorized by the account owner, JARVIS Content Creator may process information provided through supported platform APIs, including:

  • Basic authorized account and profile information.
  • Authentication and authorization tokens.
  • Content prepared or selected for publication.
  • Publishing status and platform-generated identifiers.
  • Performance and engagement information made available by authorized APIs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the authorized JARVIS Content Creator workflow, including account authorization, content preparation, approved publishing, status tracking, analytics, and related content-management functions.

4. Third-Party Platforms

JARVIS Content Creator may connect to third-party services such as social media platforms through their official APIs. Information received through those services is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the applicable platform requirements.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information obtained through authorized platform connections is not sold. Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to perform actions explicitly authorized through the JARVIS Content Creator workflow or when required by law.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials, authorization tokens, and other information processed by the service. Access is limited to the operation and maintenance of JARVIS Content Creator.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the authorized service or meet applicable legal and platform requirements. Authorized connections can be revoked, and requests concerning deletion of stored personal information can be submitted using the contact information below.

8. User Control

Users may revoke access through the applicable third-party platform where supported. Revoking authorization may prevent JARVIS Content Creator from accessing or publishing to that platform.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as JARVIS Content Creator evolves or as platform and legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy or data-related questions can be directed to the operator of Kevin.Kools.Digital through the contact information provided through its official channels.
